## Missing information (do this first)
- Before starting, list what you still need from the board: endpoints (URLs/IPs), credentials or access you lack, files, decisions. Be specific (e.g. "Prometheus URL on lxc-monitoring").
- Do not guess or invent values. If something blocks you, stop, ask, and say what you could already do without it.
- Never ask for secrets in plain text; ask where they live and which scoped, read-only credential to use.
